About CJpy

Our Mission

CJpy is an intensive, cohort-based Python programming bootcamp designed specifically for Ghanaian high school (SHS) graduates, university students, and young professionals breaking into software engineering and technology.

How We Teach

We focus 100% on Python with a project-driven curriculum. No outdated languages or passive lectures — from day one, students install VS Code, write code live, build working automation scripts and software tools, and graduate with projects they can showcase on GitHub.

Who Leads the Sessions

All sessions are taught and mentored by Electrical Engineering and Computer Science students and practitioners who actively build, ship, and debug software in Ghana. Each cohort is capped at 20 students per breakout class to ensure personalized attention.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is CJpy?

A cohort-based Python programming bootcamp for Ghanaian SHS students, uni students, and young professionals trying to break into tech. Python only — no C, no distractions. Learn. Build. Lead.

2. When does it run?

Monday, Wednesday, Friday. 3 sessions a week for 30 days. Each session is 120–150 minutes. This isn't a weekend crash course — it's a commitment, and that's the point.

3. Where do sessions happen?

Live on Zoom. All the info sharing, resources, and mentor conversations happen in our dedicated WhatsApp community — so even outside class, you're never stuck.

4. What do I need to join?

Just a laptop and internet. If you don't already have a code editor, install VS Code beforehand — it's free and it's what we'll be using.

5. How big are the classes?

Max 20 students per class, split into breakout rooms. Small enough that tutors actually know your name.

6. How much does it cost?

GH₵ 300 for the full 30 days.

7. Do I need any coding experience?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can start this.

8. Who's teaching?

EE students who've actually built and debugged real things — not just people reading slides at you.

9. What if I fall behind or miss a session?

Every session is recorded, so you don't lose the day. And your mentor is one WhatsApp message away — they'll actually talk you through what you missed, not just point you to a link.

10. Is there a refund policy?

No refunds. Not because we don't care — because we do. The moment there's an exit door, people take it the first time it gets hard, and that's exactly the week they needed to push through. We built this program for people who are in. If you're not ready to show up 3 times a week for a month, wait for a cohort when you are — we'd rather have you fully committed later than half-in now.

11. Do people actually recommend this after finishing?

Yes. The large majority of Cohort 1 students said they'd already recommended CJpy to someone else, or would. Nobody told us to say that — that's what they told us when we asked.

12. Will I get a certificate?

Yes, on completion.

13. Is this just theory or do I actually build things?

You build. That's the "Build" in Learn. Build. Lead. Lectures without shipped code don't get anyone hired.

14. I'm not a "tech person," is this still for me?

Most of Cohort 1 wasn't either. That's the whole reason CJpy exists.

15. How do I sign up?

WhatsApp us or head to joincjpy.com. Cohort 2 starts September 10.
